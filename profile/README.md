We study the security, privacy, and trustworthiness of modern AI systems, and measure empirically
how they are built and used: model provenance, generative AI, adversarial machine learning,
and protection against AI misuse.

### Research

**🔎 Attribution, provenance & auditing:** which model produced a given image, whether an image is
human-made or AI-generated, and which training data drove a model's behavior.

**🎨 Generative models & training data:** how training data shapes what generative models learn
and produce, and how edits to training images affect a model's output.

**🛡️ Security & privacy in machine learning:** detecting backdoors, defending against black-box
query attacks, and tools that limit what models can learn about people.
